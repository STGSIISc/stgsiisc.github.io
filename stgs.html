What's STGS?

We are a bunch of Graduate Students at the Dept. of Mathematics in IISc Bangalore discussing topics in Topology and Geometry.

The seminar has two modules: Topology and Geometry.

We meet Monday and Friday every week, once for each module.

The meetings are offline, 90 minutes long (approx.), and take place in LH-1 of the Department Building.

The seminar meetings are informal. As none of us is an expert in the topics presented, there are always lively discussions throughout.

We aim to explore core and active topics in the field together, cultivate questions, and pursue interesting problems.
